Sungyoon Lee

Associate Professor of Literacy Education · Department of Teaching, Learning, and Innovation, Middle Tennessee State University

Sungyoon Lee is a literacy researcher whose work examines how children and adolescents read, write and learn from texts. The work spans eye-tracking studies of reading processes, the comprehension of illustrated science texts, early writing development, technology-supported literacy instruction, and meta-analyses of reading, writing and content-area interventions. Before doctoral study at Texas A&M University (Ph.D. 2019), Sungyoon Lee taught for about a decade in elementary schools in Seoul, South Korea.
